# Changelog

## 0.5.6

- Add vault-owner lifecycle builders and RPC resolution for prices, proposals, recipients and second-leg rolls; add own-maker settlement, including vault writers, with TS instruction parity fixtures. Snapshot types now include vault ownership flags, settlement price, fee recipient and governance nominee.

- Add settlement/unlock ATA sequences, owner/staging RPC guards and four-way token-program goldens; keep general position decoding tolerant and validate flags only for settlement.

## 0.5.5

- Allow funding Open positions after market finalization.
- Breaking: `VaultView.staging` preserves the account slot; `next_step` waits for unstaging before equity or cycle finalization.

## 0.5.4

- `MakerDataClient::subscribe_messages()` receives owner-direct `PositionUpdated`, `TradeExecuted` and `MakerCapsUpdated` after summary recovery, without replacing the quote session. Up to four data sessions per owner.

- `chain`: typed `PositionSnapshot` funding/settlement builders, explicit `wsol::build_wrap_sol_ixs` wrapping and checked premium-token balance decoding. `chain-rpc`: `maker_snapshot` batches positions, premium ATAs and unique markets in chunks of 100; `maker_open_positions` discovers Open/Funded positions directly on chain for startup/manual recovery. `fetch_accounts` and `rpc()` expose batched and advanced reads.
- `chain-rpc`: `deposit_premium`, `withdraw_premium`, `fund_position` and `send_instructions_with_options` retain confirmation semantics: `Ok` requires confirmation at the client commitment and on-chain failure returns `Err`. `with_confirmation_ws_url` (`ws-client`) selects signature subscriptions; without it, conveniences retain the legacy RPC confirmation fallback. Only `send_instructions_with_blockhash` is submit-only and accepts a shared batch blockhash; `signature_statuses` checks chunks of 256. With `ws-client`, `confirm_signatures` waits on Solana signature subscriptions and reconciles unresolved signatures with one batched status pass on WS failure/deadline; pending remains unknown.

- `ws::maker::QuoteOutcomeTracker` tracks a sent order from `subscribe_messages()` without a task: pending, acknowledged, selected, typed rejection, fill details, expiry, cancellation or RFQ loss. Epoch changes, deadlines and stream gaps/closure yield `Unknown`; reconcile through `GetMyQuotes` / `GetOrderStatus` after reconnect. A filled `RfqClosed` can precede `QuoteFilled`.
- Breaking, `http`: optional `reqwest` moves from 0.12 to 0.13 (`json`, `query`, `rustls`). Rustls uses the platform verifier for system trust and the reqwest 0.13 crypto-provider selection. `HttpClient::with_client` now takes a 0.13 client.

- `VaultSwapKind::RecoveryStage` builds recovery staging halves with MAIN valuation; `verify_vault_swap` accepts served recovery requests with a stage step.
- Breaking, wire: `WelcomeData` carries `min_quote_validity_seconds` and `settlement_buffer_seconds`. `QuoteBuilder::welcome(&WelcomeData)` applies them as `venue(&Venue)` does from HTTP, without the `http` feature.
- `decimal` feature: exact `rust_decimal` views. `Strike::to_decimal`, `Price::to_decimal`, `Price::from_decimal_floor`, `Quantity::to_decimal(decimals)` and `Quantity::from_decimal_floor(units, decimals)` (rounded down; `None` when negative or out of range).
- `MakerDataClient::all_maker_positions(filter, timeout)` pages `GetMakerPositions` to the end, for an `MmSummary` with `positions_has_more`.
- Curator PT router: pure Jupiter/Exponent adapters, v0 fit and staging plans, RPC/Jupiter execution and simulation (`chain-rpc` + `http`), oracle quote screening, backend verified submission, and held-option funding needs. `VaultAccount` now exposes `active_expiry`.

- Breaking, `chain::vault`: vault prices come from `PriceRoute` accounts (`["price_route", mint]`) instead of the oracle source's Pyth feed. `PriceRoute::{parse, from_account}` decodes one, `PriceRoute::accounts(address, shard)` lists one price leg, and `VaultManager::price_section(main, second, shard)` builds the pair's section. `process_equity`, `process_idle_withdrawal` and `apply_update` take the price section instead of two price-update accounts. `VaultAddresses::{main,second}_oracle_source` become `{main,second}_price_route`; `oracle_source_pyth_feed` and `VaultChainError::MissingPythFeed` are removed (`VaultChainError::PriceRoute` is new).
- Breaking, `chain::vault`: swaps run as 1–4 hops through programs the `SwapVenues` registry (`["swap_venues"]`, `VaultAddresses::swap_venues`) lists; every swap instruction ends its fixed accounts with the registry (read-only) and each hop is `program, source, destination, accounts..`. `VaultSwapPlan { price_section, amount_in, minimum_out, valid_until, hops: Vec<VaultSwapHop> }` replaces `VaultSwapRoute`; `MAX_VAULT_SWAP_HOPS` is new, and `JUPITER_V6_PROGRAM_ID` / `DEVNET_SWAP_ROUTER_ID` are removed (the program's venue registry decides). `VaultSwapIntent` takes `price_routes: PairPriceRoutes { main, second, shards }` instead of `price_feeds` and `router`; each served price leg must equal its route's accounts on a configured shard (`VaultIntentError::PriceSection` replaces `PriceUpdate`), the served hops must chain the input leg through transit ATAs to the output leg, a leg ATA may appear only as the first source or last destination, a hop may carry the vault's canonical ATAs of other mints as scratch but never the share mint or the vault's share ATA, and no hop account may make a fixed or hop-program account writable, except the pair and staging mints (an Exponent `merge` burns the PT mint, an SY mint or redeem writes the staging mint; the vault is never a mint authority) and the price section (the program reads both prices before the first hop). Between the compute budget and the swap the served transaction may carry setup: an idempotent ATA the fee payer creates for the vault on a mint other than its legs and share mint (transit or scratch), and `UpdateStakePoolBalance` on the SPL or Sanctum multi-validator stake-pool program for a pool one of the vault's price routes pins; anything else fails with `VaultIntentError::Setup` (new). `amount_in` is the most the input leg may spend; minimum output and drift apply to the actual spend and gain.
- `http`: `Vault.staging: Option<VaultStaging { mint, from_leg: VaultLeg, amount, staged_at, generation }>` is the leg balance the vault holds in another mint's ATA, pending return to a leg; `generation` names the open slot.
- Staged swaps: `SwapQuoteResultData.staging: Option<VaultStagingStep>` (`stage` / `unstage`) marks half of a swap split over two transactions through a staging mint; after a confirmed stage, request the same swap again for the unstage. `VaultSwapKind::Staging { step, leg: VaultLeg, asset: VaultStagingAsset { mint, token_program } }` builds `VaultStagingSwap` (price section: the leg's route, then the staging mint's). Breaking: `PairPriceRoutes.staging: &[PriceRoute]` lists the staging mints the operator accepts (empty refuses staged swaps). `verify_vault_swap` checks a stage against the request's input leg and amount, and an unstage against its output leg; an unstage's input is what was staged, and `min_output_floor` does not apply to a stage. A hop may write the staging mint.
- Breaking, staged swaps v2: `VaultStagingSwap`'s header is 36 bytes (`generation u64` at 27, `hop_count` at 35) and `VaultSwapKind::Staging` carries `generation` (the vault's `staged_generation + 1` to stage, the open slot's generation to unstage; the program refuses others with 1106 `VaultStagingGenerationMismatch`). `VaultAccount.staging: VaultStagingState { generation, open: Option<VaultStagedSlot { mint, from_leg, amount, staged_at }> }` decodes the slot; a nonzero `staged_kind` fails with `VaultChainError::UnknownStagedKind`, an unknown staged leg with `UnknownStagedLeg`. `VaultSwapIntent.account` is the vault as read before the request: `verify_vault_swap` checks the staging half against its slot and refuses a stage while a slot is open (no top-ups), an unstage with nothing staged, of another mint or of less or more than the whole staged amount, and a served generation other than the slot's.
- Price-route bricks: `PriceRoute` is 688 bytes and decodes into `PriceRoute { mint, max_skew_secs, bricks: Vec<PriceBrick> }`, at most `MAX_PRICE_BRICKS` (6) of `PriceBrick::{Pyth(PythBrick { feed_id, max_age_secs, min_band_ppm }), AccountRatio(AccountRatioBrick), MaturityDiscount(MaturityDiscountBrick), ClampMax { max }, GrowthCap(GrowthCapBrick)}`. A leg's accounts are the route, then per brick the Pyth push-oracle account on the shard or an `AccountRatio`'s account and its owner's `ProgramData` (a PT leg: Exponent vault, Exponent `ProgramData`, pool, pool `ProgramData`); a route whose bricks need other than its `account_count` accounts fails to parse. A served `UpdateStakePoolBalance` must name the account of one of the routes' `AccountRatio` bricks.
- Breaking, second-leg roll: `VaultAccount.second_leg_generation` counts applied rolls and `VaultManagerArgs.second_leg_generation` names the generation its `second_mint` belongs to. `VaultManager::ensure_current(&VaultAccount)` fails with `VaultChainError::SecondLegRolled` once the vault's second leg moved on, and `VaultManager::refresh(&VaultAccount)` rebuilds the second mint, ATA and price route (returns whether it rolled). `VaultSwapIntent.account: VaultAccount` replaces `staging`, and `verify_vault_swap` refuses a manager the vault rolled past. `VaultGovernanceUpdate::SecondLeg(new_mint)` (kind 6) is proposed with `propose_update` and applied with `VaultManager::apply_second_leg(proposal_payer, new_mint, price_section)` (the actor signs and pays the new ATA; pass the price section only while the old second ATA holds dust); `apply_update` refuses kind 6.
- Wire: `SwapQuoteResultData` drops `main_price_update` / `second_price_update`; the served transaction carries the price section.
- Breaking, `chain::vault`: a hop's wire header is `account_count u8 | data_len u16 | amount_offset u16` (`u16::MAX` = none). `VaultSwapHop::amount_offset: Option<VaultSwapAmountOffset { offset, num, den }>` is new: on a hop after the first, the program writes `floor(((received + 1) * num - 1) / den)` (the amount its source received earlier in the swap, scaled; `num == den` writes it unchanged) as u64 LE at `data[offset..offset + 8]`; a set offset is followed on the wire by `scale_num u64 | scale_den u64`, and both must be nonzero. `MAX_VAULT_SWAP_ROUTE_ACCOUNTS` (64) now caps CPI accounts per hop; `MAX_VAULT_SWAP_PATH_ACCOUNTS` (128, new) caps the sum over hops of CPI accounts plus 4, duplicates counted.
