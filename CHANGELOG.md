# Changelog

## 0.5.2

- Breaking: `ManagedWsConfig::new(url, hello, signer, auth_pubkey)` and `new_async(url, hello, signer, auth_pubkey)` take the maker owner explicitly; `with_auth_pubkey` is removed. The signer must be the owner's registered quote-signing key (which may equal the owner key).
- Breaking: `QuoteBuilder` requires `.maker_owner(owner)` (no signer-key fallback) and `.min_valid_until(now + min_quote_validity_seconds)`; missing values are `QuoteBuildError::Missing`. `valid_until` before the floor fails with `ValidUntilTooSoon`, after the market expiry with `ValidUntilAfterMarketExpiry`. `preimage_args()` takes no argument. `RfqBinding::market_expiry()` is new.
- Breaking: `MakerQuoteClient` quotes only in an explicit epoch: `quote_in_epoch`, `replace_in_epoch`, `batch_quotes_in_epoch` and their `try_` forms replace `quote`, `replace_quote`, `batch_quotes`. `ManagedWsHandle::try_send_in_epoch` is new. `VaultOperatorClient` likewise replaces `quote`, `try_quote`, `replace_quote`, `batch_quotes` with `quote_in_epoch`, `try_quote_in_epoch`, `replace_in_epoch`, `batch_quotes_in_epoch`.
- Breaking: `ManagedWsEvent::Disconnected { close: Option<ServerClose> }` carries the server's close frame; `WsClient::server_close()` returns it after `next()` ends. `ServerClose { code, reason }` keeps the raw code; `close_code()` decodes it to `Option<CloseCode>`. After a server error frame that ends the session (e.g. `auth_timeout`, `session_replaced`), the managed client leaves `Ready`, stops writing without sending its own Close, and waits up to one second for the server's close frame outside the auth and readiness deadlines. Every opened connection emits exactly one `Disconnected`, before `Terminated` when the client stops, including `close()` and a session panic.
- Recovery reads follow the endpoint: `/maker` reads `GetActiveRfqs` and `GetMyQuotes { scope: Live }`, `/maker/data` reads `GetMmSummary`; `/vault` is unchanged.
- `broadcast_buffer` defaults to 4096 (was 64). A gap on the quote endpoint reconnects, and cancel-on-disconnect then removes the session's active and retained non-winning quotes; selected and executing orders keep their lifecycle.
- `chain::ix::build_update_quote_signing_ixs` with `UpdateQuoteSigningIxArgs` builds `UpdateMakerQuoteSigning` for key rotation.
- Wire: `QuoteRejectReason::{RateLimited, TradingPaused}`; `AuthRequiredAction::ReadMakerData`; `DbFeature::{MmSummary, MyQuotes, MyTrades}`; `CloseCode` (`from_code`, `code`, `reason`) for server close frames 4000–4005; `SwapQuoteResultData` carries `main_price_update` / `second_price_update`; `WsChannel::Positions` is removed.
- Breaking, wire: `RfqRequest` (replacing `client_request_id`, still the idempotency key), `AcceptQuote` and `SubmitSignedSponsoredTx` require `request_id`; each gets one `ServerMessage::CommandAccepted(CommandAcceptedData { request_id })` or `RequestError`, and `send_await` correlates both (an `RfqRequest` no longer waits for `RfqCreated`). `RfqCreatedData.client_request_id` is renamed `request_id`. `RequestErrorEnvelope.delivery: Option<Delivery>` (`NotForwarded` / `Unknown`) marks submission failures; `NotForwarded` covers only that attempt. `ServerError::Generic.code` and `AuthErrorData.reason` are `WsErrorCode`.
- Breaking, `vault`: `VaultOperatorClient::rfq_request`, `accept_quote` and `submit_signed_sponsored_tx` return `Result<(), VaultRequestError>` and resolve on the receipt; `VaultRequestError::delivery()` exposes the rejection's delivery. `swap_status(execution_id)` sends `GetSwapStatus` and returns `SwapStatusMessage { state: SwapExecutionState }`; terminal outcomes are also pushed as `SwapSettled`.
- Wire, breaking: `SponsoredTxToSignData` and `SwapQuoteResultData` carry `last_valid_block_height: u64` (JSON number), the exact height the served transaction's blockhash is valid through.
- `vault` + `http`: `VaultFleet::discover(VaultFleetConfig)` follows the vault catalog for one operator key — a `/taker` session subscribed to `vaults` re-lists on each (re)subscription and `VaultCatalogChanged`, never on a timer — and publishes `FleetListing::{Pending, Listed, Failed}` on `listings()`. `Listed` is published only while the feed is live: a feed that does not subscribe within `with_timeout` (default 5 s), or that stays silent for two liveness probes (`Ping` every timeout), publishes `Failed` and reconnects with backoff from `with_retry` (default 5 s) up to 60 s; a failed listing retries after `retry`. Every write and each listing are bounded by the timeout, listings run alongside the socket, and a listing still in flight when the feed dies is dropped unpublished. Both intervals are clamped to 100 ms..=60 s. `HttpClient::delegated_vaults(delegate)` reads `GET /api/v1/vaults?delegate=` (current, unexpired grants only). `ws::auth::validate_wallet_auth_challenge` checks a `StartAuth` challenge.
- `vault` + `chain-rpc`: `VaultOperatorClient::sell_rfq(chain, &VaultSale, epoch, gate, &mut impl SaleDesk)` drives a vault sale: `RfqRequest`, quotes offered to `SaleDesk::take`, `AcceptQuote`, the served transaction verified against the counterparty loaded from chain and signed, `SubmitSignedSponsoredTx`. It returns `SaleOutcome::{Unfilled, Submitted(SignedSale)}`; `SignedSale` has the transaction id and served `last_valid_block_height`. `SaleError::in_flight()` returns the signed sale a failure may have left in flight. The market PDA must be canonical for `VaultSale::market`. `VaultSale::window` bounds quote collection including `take`, then serving plus `writer`, verification and signing; a stalled hook ends with `SaleError::TimedOut(SaleStage::{Take, Sign})` before anything is signed.
- `SendGate` / `Ungated`: the bot's admission for driver sends, enqueued under the gate's lock. Breaking: `swap_submit` is replaced by `swap_submit_in_epoch(payload, epoch, gate)`. `VaultRequestError::Gated` is new, and `delivery()` also classifies local failures: refusals before the write are `NotForwarded`, possibly written commands `Unknown`.
- `chain`: `Counterparty::load(&ChainClient, owner)` (`chain-rpc`) resolves a quote's maker as a holder vault or an external maker. `MarketInfo`, `MakerInfo` and `PositionInfo` decode with `from_account(&Account, &program_id)`, checking the owner and header; market and maker also check the exact layout length and version 1. Breaking: `MarketInfo` gains `expiry_ts`, `is_put` and `is_finalized`, and rejects unknown flags. `VaultAccount::from_account` checks the owner, and `VaultAccount` decodes `open_positions`. `ChainError::{Vault, Identity}` and `VaultChainError::Owner` are new. The account decoders need `chain`, not `chain-rpc`.
- Breaking, `chain::vault`: `VaultSwapIntent` takes `price_feeds: PairPriceFeeds { main, second, shards }` instead of explicit price-update accounts. The served `main_price_update` / `second_price_update` must be a configured shard of the pair's feed (`VaultIntentError::PriceUpdate`) and are verified once.
- `chain-rpc`: `ChainClient::unfunded_positions(&vault)` returns the vault's maker positions still `Open`, and `pending_capital_requests(&vault)` returns its nonzero queued deposits and withdrawals (`chain::vault::PendingCapitalRequests`). Both read program accounts (`getProgramAccounts` with size and memcmp filters on the vault) and decode each with the checked decoders; a malformed account is an error. `VaultRequest::from_account` checks owner and size. Breaking: `PositionInfo::from_account` requires the current 424-byte, version-2 layout.
- Maker-authenticated network examples require `ACTA_MAKER_OWNER`; the quoting examples read the minimum quote validity from `GET /api/v1/venue` and need the `http` feature.

## 0.5.0

- `ServerMessage::VaultCatalogChanged(VaultCatalogChangedData { vault_pda })` goes to `vaults` subscribers after a vault is created, re-delegated or closed; re-list vaults on it.
- Breaking: `VaultSwapRoute` and `VaultSwapIntent` take `router`, the swap router pinned by the target program build (`JUPITER_V6_PROGRAM_ID`; the devnet build pins `DEVNET_SWAP_ROUTER_ID`).
- Breaking: vault instruction opcodes follow the contract's contiguous renumbering; instructions built by 0.4.3-vaults.1 are invalid against the current program.
- Removed, breaking: vault signing has one path. `VaultOperatorClient::sign_sponsored_tx` / `sign_swap` (byte-equality against an expected message), `sign_sponsored_tx_unverified` / `sign_swap_unverified`, `ws::vault::sign_transaction_base64` / `sign_transaction_base64_unverified`, `ensure_deadline_ahead` and `VaultTxError::{InvalidBase64, MessageMismatch, Malformed, UnsupportedVersion, SignerNotRequired}` are gone. Transaction decoding errors arrive as `VaultTxError::Intent(VaultIntentError::…)`, and `VaultIntentError::SignerNotRequired` is new.
- Features, breaking: `vault-chain` is removed. `chain::vault` comes with `chain` (which now pulls `base64`, not the WebSocket stack); `vault` implies `chain`.
- `VaultManager`, breaking: `process_deposits` takes any iterator of `impl Into<DepositPayout>` and `process_withdrawals` one of `impl Into<WithdrawalPayout>`, so `&[Pubkey]` works as is. `process_final_withdrawal` takes `impl Into<WithdrawalPayout>`. Override a blocked canonical ATA with `DepositPayout::new(user).refund_to(..)` / `.shares_to(..)` and `WithdrawalPayout::new(user).shares_back_to(..)`. `share_return_account(kind, user)` gives the fallback share account the program creates with seed under the actor. The `_with_destinations` / `_with_share_destination(s)` builders are removed. `deposit_batch_shape_with_destinations` / `withdrawal_batch_shape_with_destinations` are renamed `deposit_batch_shape` / `withdrawal_batch_shape`.
- `VaultAccount` decodes the 712-byte vault: `capital_fallback_delay_secs`, `closing_started_at`, `boundary_equity` and `boundary_shares`. Deposits and withdrawals are priced at the boundary pair; `last_share_price` is display-only.
- Reference data, breaking: `HttpClient::venue()` (`GET /api/v1/venue`: program id, minimum quote validity, settlement buffer, quote refresh lead, RFQ timeout bounds, signature timeout, per-RFQ and per-taker limits, pre-expiry cutoff), `HttpClient::tokens()` (`GET /api/v1/tokens`, `BTreeMap<mint, TokenInfo>`) and `HttpClient::markets(&MarketFilter)` (`GET /api/v1/markets?underlying=&quote=&expiry=&is_put=&active=`, `Vec<MarketInfo>`; `active` defaults to `true` on the server; `active: Some(false)` returns every market the server holds in memory, so finalized history covers only markets finalized since the server started.) Reads revalidate with `If-None-Match` and reuse the cached body on 304, so bots can poll freely. `http::Market`, `http::Tokens` and `http::TokenDetails` are removed; `HttpError::Decode` is new.
- `TokenInfo` is one shape on both transports: `mint`, `symbol`, `decimals`, optional `oracle_feed_ids`; underlyings add `size_rule`, `strike_tick` and `quote_mints`. `size_rule` is now `Option`. WS `TokensData` carries `tokens: Vec<TokenInfo>` (no `underlyings` / `quotes_by_underlying`). `MarketInfo` gains `status` (`MarketStatus::{Active, Closed, Finalized}`) and optional `settlement_price`.
- `GetTokensMessage` and `GetMarketDescriptorsMessage` lose `active_only`: the server always answers from its active snapshot. `default_true` is removed.
- `ServerMessage::ReferenceChanged(ReferenceChangedData { tokens_etag, markets_etag })` is pushed to every session when a reference snapshot changes; `subscribe_reference_changes()` on `ManagedWsHandle`, the maker data and quote clients and `VaultOperatorClient` yields only these.
- Checked signing for server-built vault transactions (`vault`): `VaultOperatorClient::verify_and_sign_sponsored_tx` and `verify_and_sign_swap`, plus `chain::vault::verify_vault_open(&SponsoredTxToSignData, …)` / `verify_vault_swap`, which return a `VerifiedTransaction` to `sign`. The expected instructions are rebuilt locally from `VaultOpenIntent` (`Sale` = `OpenPositionForVaultAsTaker`, `BetweenVaults` = `OpenPositionBetweenVaults`, `Fill` = `OpenPositionForVaultAsMaker`) with `VaultTradeTerms`, or from `VaultSwapIntent` (`VaultSpotRebalance`, `RecoveryConvertToMain`, `ClosingConvertToMain`); a served minimum output below the operator's own `min_output_floor` is rejected even when it fits the quote's slippage. Fee payer, signers, compute budget (`ComputeBudgetBounds`), every account with its signer/writable flags, data, canonical idempotent ATA creates, the maker's Ed25519 quote signature and the bound order id must match; router route accounts are accepted only in the swap's route tail (at most 64, no signer, no payer, no read-only vault account made writable). v0 lookup tables resolve from the tables passed in; `ChainClient::address_lookup_tables` fetches active ones and fails with `ChainError::MissingLookupTable` or `ChainError::LookupTableDeactivated`. `VaultIntentError` names the first mismatch.
- `VaultManager::fund_maker_position(&VaultPositionRef)` encodes `VaultFundMakerPosition` (opcode 43) for a position the vault holds; the funding mint is the settlement leg (call: quote, put: underlying) and the vault ATA is picked by mint.
- Swap verification compares the served minimum output with `floor(quoted_out * (10000 - slippage_bps) / 10000)`, the router's rounding; quotes whose division leaves a remainder no longer fail with "minimum output exceeds the requested slippage".
- `VaultManager` encodes `open_position_as_taker`, `open_position_as_maker`, `open_position_with_vault_holder` and `swap` in the current contract layouts (legs picked by mint; mint/program slots in market order).

## 0.4.3-vaults.1

- The `http` feature adds `http::HttpClient`, a read-only client for the public `/api/v1/vaults`, `/api/v1/vaults/{pda}` and `/api/v1/markets` routes with wire-exact `Vault` and `Market` types.
- `can_maker()` accepts governance with a current `SIGN_MAKER_QUOTES` grant. The backend requires an active primary-delegate grant even when governance and delegate share a key; revoked or expired grants cannot quote.

- `SwapQuoteData` carries `direction: VaultSwapDirection` (`main_to_second` / `second_to_main`) instead of `input_mint` / `output_mint`.
- `RfqRequest`, `AcceptQuote`, `SubmitSignedSponsoredTx` and `CancelRfq` now wait for a Ready session like other trading messages.
- The `vault-chain` feature adds `chain::vault`: `VaultManager` builds `SetAcceptingDeposits`, `ProcessVaultEquity` (and the idle-withdrawal variant), `ProcessDeposits`, `ProcessWithdrawals`, `ProcessFinalWithdrawal` and `FinalizeVaultCycle`; batches are capped at `VAULT_CAPITAL_BATCH_USERS` (three users fit the 1232-byte packet) and sized with `vault_capital_batch_compute_units` over `deposit_batch_shape_with_destinations` / `withdrawal_batch_shape_with_destinations`.
- Vault governance instructions: `propose_update`, `apply_update`, `cancel_update`, `rotate_delegate`, `disable_delegate`.
- `VaultAccount` / `VaultRequest` decoders, `oracle_source_pyth_feed` and `pyth_price_feed_account`.
- Crank decision `next_step`, `WindowTracker` and `plan_withdrawals`; `examples/vault_capital.rs` runs the cycle over RPC.
- Checked transaction signing decodes once, compares the locally approved message before deadlines, and preserves other signature slots for legacy/v0 transactions.
- `VaultSwapMode::Closing` encodes `closing` for governance-controlled terminal second-to-main conversion; normal maker/RFQ messages are unchanged.
- Projection adjacency rejects older, duplicate and wrapped versions; the operator example keeps its cursor monotonic.
- Concurrent vault swap failures retain their own correlation, including out-of-order `trading_paused`.
- Vault grants clear on connection end, failed recovery, or task panic. `VaultOperatorClient::can_maker()` / `can_taker()` require Ready and the current connection grant; reconnect replaces grants.
- The `vault` feature adds `VaultOperatorConfig` / `VaultOperatorClient` on `/vault`: `StartVaultAuth` signs with the operator key on each reconnect (never `ResumeAuth`); taker reads reconcile on every auth and maker reads when the delegate may quote; `sign_quote` uses the vault as `maker_owner`; `sign_sponsored_tx` / `sign_swap` check the expected message and reject expired deadlines locally (`_unverified` variants skip the message check); `swap_quote` and `swap_submit` correlate; `subscribe_projections()` streams `VaultProjectionAdvanced`; `MakerWsEndpoint::Vault` and `ManagedWsHandle::vault_auth()` expose the path.
- Vault-only wire types: `StartVaultAuth`, `SwapQuote`, `SwapSubmit` (`ClientMessage`); `VaultAuthSuccess`, `VaultProjectionAdvanced`, `SwapQuoteResult`, `SwapSubmitResult` (`ServerMessage`); `WsEndpointKind::Vault`, `WsChannel::Vaults`, `VaultOperatorKind`, `VaultDelegatePermissions`, `VaultSwapMode`, and `VaultSwapDirection`. Makers are unchanged.

## 0.4.3 - 2026-09-18

- Native SOL funding reads the maker and position ATAs in one RPC when both are needed. Rent, fee reserve and final wallet-budget checks are preserved.
- Settlement amount calculation cancels decimal scales before multiplication, avoiding intermediate overflow when the result fits in `u64`.
- Decode the on-chain `Closed` position status.
- Add optional `GlobalStats.usd` with separate notional and premium totals as decimal dollar strings, plus the number of priced confirmed trades. Older snapshots without USD values remain readable.
- Network examples load a Solana keypair file, support a separate maker owner, and complete the Hello → Welcome → StartAuth handshake. README includes setup and run commands.

- `QuoteExpiry::to_system_time` returns `Option<SystemTime>` and `From<QuoteExpiry> for SystemTime` is removed. Use `expiry.to_system_time().ok_or(...)` instead of an infallible conversion. Out-of-range wire timestamps retain their exact u64 value and no longer panic; `remaining_from` returns `None` for them.

- Derive and verify the RFQ market PDA before quote signing, including program, chain, option type and collateral/settlement mints. `RfqBinding::from_broadcast` uses the SDK's Acta program; `from_broadcast_for_program` accepts an explicitly configured deployment ID. Neither path trusts the endpoint to select the program.
- Validate the canonical maker authentication challenge before calling local or asynchronous signers, including reconnects and mid-session challenges. Raw-client examples use the same validator. Order signing bytes are unchanged.
- Decode referral counters as `u64`, matching the server and accepting its full range. Client code with explicit `i64` annotations must update those annotations.
- Ignore unknown subscription channels during recovery instead of sending `Unsubscribe("unknown")`. Unknown trading states still reject.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-08-31

- **Breaking:** `Welcome.server_time_unix_ms`, `AuthSuccess.expires_at`, `RfqBroadcast.sent_at_unix_ms` (server emission time), and `instruction_index` on all six known chain events are required; missing/null values reject. Timestamp units and protocol version are unchanged; credential expiry applies to resume, not socket lifetime. Chain-event identity is `(signature, instruction_index)`; only unknown variants lack an SDK identity.
- Quotes and cancellations share FIFO. `CancelQuoteAck` requires `request_id`, `rfq_id`, and `cancelled_order_ids`; lifecycle `QuoteCancelled` never resolves cancellation awaits. `CancelRfq` is send-only: `send_await` returns `NoCorrelationKey`, and `RfqClosed` is lifecycle evidence.
- Quote clients default to COD. `with_cancel_on_disconnect(true)` requests `cancel_on_disconnect`; absent `Welcome` support terminates with `FeatureUnsupported`, and `QuoteCancelReason::MakerDisconnected` decodes it. `GapPolicy::EndpointDefault` reconnects Quote sessions and surfaces Data gaps; explicit policies and `low_latency()` remain available. COD runs after server disconnect handling.
- CancelAll and COD remove retained non-winners in locked RFQs; removed orders cannot return through rollback, selected orders remain, and single-RFQ CancelQuote rejects locked RFQs.
- **Breaking:** `auto_reconcile` and `with_auto_reconcile` are removed. Recovery is required before Ready; commands submitted from Ready, including raw calls and queries, bind to its connection epoch. Typed data requests capture readiness and epoch together.
- Desired subscriptions, including removals and empty mint scopes, survive reconnect, resume, and fresh auth; rejected changes roll back. A second pending mutation returns `SubscriptionPending`; trading mutations before Ready or from a stale epoch return `NotReady`; subscription ACK timeouts start after write.
- `initial_subscribe` defines the desired target, not a wire request: recovery uses a new request ID and explicitly replaces both mint scopes, including empty scopes.
- Lifecycle `rfq_version`, `order_version`, and `QuoteCancelled.order_ids` are required; malformed values no longer default to zero or empty.
- `GetMyQuotes.scope` is required: unpaged `Live` is the complete owner set and paginated `History` is separate. `MakerQuoteInfo.state` is required: `Active { rank, rfq_version }`, `Retained { rfq_version }`, `AwaitingSignature { rfq_version }`, `Executing { rfq_version }`, or `Historical { status }`; missing fields reject and `Default` remains explicit Live.
- `MakerDataClient` adds typed `GetOrderStatus`: `Pending`, `Confirmed { position_pda }`, or `Unknown`; `Unknown` does not prove no execution. `ActiveRfqInfo.taker` is part of the `order_id` preimage, allowing reconnect recovery from `GetActiveRfqs`.
- A separate writer prioritizes control frames over the FIFO data lane while reads continue. Stalled writes fail their ticket within `write_timeout` and reconnect; full lanes return `QueueFull` from `send()`/`send_await()`.
- `ManagedWsConfig::low_latency()` uses 2 s ping/pong, 1 s writes, larger queues, and `GapPolicy::Reconnect`.
- **Breaking:** `ManagedWsConfig::new` takes `Arc<dyn SignerLike + Send + Sync>` instead of a challenge closure and pubkey; it defaults to the signer key, `with_auth_pubkey` sets a delegating owner, and `ChallengeSigner` is removed.
- `UnrenderablePositionInfo.created_at` is required. `QuoteBuilder::maker_owner()` and `ManagedWsConfig::with_auth_pubkey()` support delegated quote/auth keys without `order_id preimage mismatch`.
- A session-task panic publishes `Closed { reason: SessionPanicked }` and `Terminated`, then reaches `close()`. `SendTicket` is `#[must_use]`; dropping it before dequeue cancels its send.
- `RateLimitReason::TooManyFailedOrdersPerRfq` is recognized; `AuthRequiredAction::QueryQuotes` is removed.
- `GetMakerPositions` and `GetMyQuotes` add keyset `cursor`/`cursor_id` pagination from the prior row's `(created_at, pda/order_id)`, avoiding the 500-row server clamp. `MyQuotes` adds `has_more`; unrenderable positions add `created_at`.
- `invalid_signature` retries with backoff and becomes terminal after three consecutive rejections; `maker_not_registered`, `invalid_pubkey`, `blacklisted`, and `session_replaced` remain terminal. An authenticated reconnect resumes with the server session and reaches Ready in a new epoch.
- `QuoteRejected.reason` is `CapExceeded(CapError)` with `current`/`limit`; `cap_code` and `cap_detail` are removed. `RfqSkipped` retains `cap_detail`; `CapError` adds retryable `CapsUnavailable` and raw-payload `Unknown` fallback.
- MSRV is Rust 1.89. `valid_until` is `QuoteExpiry` with whole Unix seconds on `QuoteMessage`, `ReplaceQuoteMessage`, `QuoteReceivedMessage`, `MakerQuoteInfo`, and `QuoteRefreshRequested::min_valid_until`; the wire format is unchanged and `SystemTime` truncates to the signed second.
- `MakerResponse` owns its payload; `payload()` is infallible, `into_payload()` consumes it, and `subscribe_messages()` exposes whole messages. `RfqBinding` and `QuoteBuilder` derive signed preimages and wire quotes from one RFQ/strike set; unavailable strikes fail locally.
- All timestamp serialization matches backend truncation: `TimestampSeconds<i64>` no longer rounds 36 further fields, filters, or `GetMyTrades` cursors.
- `broadcast_buffer` is slot-bounded by parsed `Arc<ServerMessage>` values and can grow beyond the old 64-slot / 128 MiB envelope. `Hello`, `StartAuth`, and initial subscriptions use fallible write paths; unserializable subscriptions are validation errors.
- `QuoteMessage::into_replacement(old_order_id)` supports HSM/remote signers and replaces local-key-only `sign_replacing`. Closing interrupts authentication and readiness waits.
- Added `MakerQuoteClient`, typed `MakerDataClient` responses, and `Connected → Authenticated → Reconciled/Ready`. Managed sends serialize once and report socket-write completion; limits match 32 KiB messages and 50-quote batches.
- Added connect deadlines, reconnect limits/jitter, session resume, and terminal rejection/replacement handling. First subscribers receive a bounded since-spawn backlog; inbound payloads share `Arc<ServerMessage>`. Unknown messages/errors/nested enums remain connected, and future batch results retain status and `order_id` correlation.
- Managed sessions fairly serve inbound traffic under sustained sends, reject oversized initial subscriptions, and stop when the last handle drops.
- **Breaking:** `ChainClient` RPC helpers are async and signer references require `Sync`; configure initial subscriptions with `with_initial_subscribe()`; `ManagedMessageReceiver::recv()` returns owned `ManagedInbound`; default features are empty and Tokio multi-thread is optional; extensible public enums are non-exhaustive and require fallback match arms.
